# Security Policy

This project is for lawful testing of your own systems, devices and lab environments.

## Allowed scope

- your own network;
- your own access points;
- your own clients;
- your own RF devices;
- isolated lab VLANs and benches;
- devices with explicit permission.

## Not allowed

- attacking third-party networks;
- intercepting communications without authorization;
- jamming or denial of service outside a controlled lab;
- bypassing rolling-code or access-control systems you do not own;
- credential theft;
- stealth or persistence tooling.

## Reporting

If you find a security issue in this repository, open a private discussion or contact the maintainer before public disclosure.
