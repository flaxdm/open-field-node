# RF Guidelines

The platform may combine Wi-Fi, LTE, LoRa, SubGHz, DMR, SDR and GPS. These must not be treated as one shared antenna block.

## Rules

- separate antennas;
- proper filtering;
- shielded RF sections where needed;
- controlled transmit power;
- physical RF enable/disable;
- keep noisy digital sections away from RF front-ends;
- use quality grounds and shielding;
- document antenna placement.

## Safety

RF transmit features are for your own devices, your own network or a shielded lab.
